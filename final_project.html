Introduction

In this project, I aim to analyze water quality data, especially emphasizing on how water temperature relates to other water quality indicators such as dissolved oxygen levels. Additionally I will explore whether whether water temperature varies significantly across different years.

Question 1: What is the relationship between water temperature and dissolved oxygen levels?

Question 2: Does water temperature vary significantly across different years?

Data Description

This data set contains the following variables:

  • Site_Id: Categorical Nominal
  • Unit_Id: Categorical Nominal
  • Read_Date: Date
  • Salinity (ppt): Numerical Continuous
  • Dissolved Oxygen (mg/L): Numerical Continuous
  • pH (standard units): Numerical Continuous
  • Secchi Depth (m): Numerical Continuous
  • Water Depth (m): Numerical Continuous
  • Water Temp (?C): Numerical Continuous
  • Air Temp-Celsius: Numerical Continuous
  • Air Temp (?F): Numerical Continuous
  • Time (24:00): Categorical Nominal
  • Field_Tech: Categorical Nominal
  • DateVerified: Date
  • WhoVerified: Categorical Nominal
  • AirTemp (C): Numerical Continuous
  • Year: Categorical Ordinal

ANOVA test to compare water temperature across all years

aov_result <- aov(`Water Temp (?C)` ~ Year, data = water_quality)

For the Inferential Statistics, I chose ANOVA test as it is ideal when comparing the means of more than two groups. In my case I am comparing water temperature across multiple years, hence ANOVA test can be used to showcase any significant difference across the years.

Display the summary of the ANOVA result

summary(aov_result)

Interpretation of ANOVA Results

Based on the results of the ANOVA test, with a p-value (0.499) > 0.05, we fail to reject the null hypothesis. This means that, based on the data, there isn’t a strong evidence to suggest that water temperatures differ significantly between the years in the dataset. This idea is further supported by F-value of 0.457 showing variation between the years is relatively small compared to variation within the years.

Conclusion

Through this project, I analyzed the relationship between water temperature and dissolved oxygen levels, as well as the variation of water temperature across different years. To do this I first explored the distribution of water temperature through a histogram, which showed that most observations fall between 20 degree celsius and 30 degree celsius, with a few extreme outliers. I created a boxplot to examine water temperature variations across different years, which showed relatively similar spread of values each year, with few outliers. Additionally, I conducted a scatterplot analysis to visualize the relationship between water temperature and dissolved oxygen, observing that dissolved oxygen levels tend to decrease slightly as water temperature increases, answering my first question on the relation between water temperature and dissolved oxygen levels.

For the inferential statistics part, I used ANOVA to test if there were significant differences in water temperatures across different years. The results of the ANOVA test showed that the p-value was 0.499, which is greater than the typical significance threshold of 0.05. This indicates that there is no statistically significant difference in water temperatures across the years in the dataset. Based on this, I concluded that water temperature does not vary significantly between the years examined, answering my second question on does water temperature significantly vary across years. Overall, the analysis provides insights into the distribution of water temperature and its relationship with other variables, however considering the limitations of the analysis (missing values, and outliers), a more in-depth investigation could reveal more trends and relationships.
